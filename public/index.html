InvoicePilot
PayPal AI Hackathon 2026

Describe the job in plain language. InvoicePilot turns it into an invoice, sends it through PayPal, and chases late payers — drafting a reminder that only sends when you approve it.

  1. 1Parse

    Plain language in, invoice fields out.

  2. 2Send

    Created and sent via PayPal sandbox.

  3. 3Detect

    Overdue invoices flagged automatically.

  4. 4Approve

    One click sends the reminder. Nothing else does.

1Describe the job

Write it the way you would say it to a client. The parser fills in the invoice below.

2Check and send the invoice

Review the fields, then create and send the invoice through PayPal sandbox.

3Check status

Look up any invoice by its ID and see whether it is paid, sent, or overdue.

4Chase an overdue invoice

For an overdue invoice, draft a reminder — AI first, a plain template if the model is down, and the draft always says which. Approving is the only thing that sends it.

InvoicePilot runs on the PayPal Invoicing API v2 (sandbox) and an OpenAI-compatible AI model. No invoice is sent and no reminder goes out without your approval. Sandbox demo — no real money moves.
